Version: 1.0.0
Date: 23. 08. 2026
  Major Features:
    - Initial release. A client-side overlay that paints where a chosen mob can stand and walk from a wand-selected block.
    - Marks inescapable holes and hazards (water, lava, soul sand, magma).
    - Built-in mobs such as Player, Ravager, and Warden, plus custom profiles (width, height, and vertical reach).
    - Colors, hazard markers, the wand item, and visibility are all configurable.
